About KAIRO ART STUDIO

KAIRO ART STUDIO is a digital painting, manga/comic creation, and frame-by-frame 2D animation studio engineered with a unified SVG icon system, 27 drawing tools, 20 brush presets, 17 blend modes, onion skinning, audio sync, and standalone Windows offline packaging.

Native project format: .kas · Export formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, Animated GIF, PNG Sequence.
